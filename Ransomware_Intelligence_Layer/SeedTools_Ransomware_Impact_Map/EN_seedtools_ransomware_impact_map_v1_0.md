# **SeedTools Ransomware Impact Map v1.0**
*(SeedTools Enterprise — Ransomware Impact & Damage Analysis Module)*

## 🎯 Module Purpose
The SeedTools Ransomware Impact Map v1.0 is a **ransomware impact map**, presenting:

- scale of damage,
- geographic scope,
- infrastructure impact,
- impact on the public and private sectors,
- financial impact,
- operational impact,
- societal impact,
- connections to ransomware families (RX Series).

The module integrates:

- **SeedTools Ransomware History Exhibit**
- **SeedTools Ransomware Behavior Matrix**
- **SeedTools Ransomware Vector Map**
- **SeedTools Encryption Timeline**
- **SeedTools Security Model**

---

# **📊 Section 1 - Impact Dimensions**

Each ransomware family is rated in 6 categories:

1. **Operational Impact** - system downtime, business interruptions

2. **Financial Impact** - costs, losses, ransom

3. **Infrastructure Impact** - impact on hospitals, transportation, energy

4. **Geographic Impact** - local, regional, global

5. **Data Impact** - encryption, leaks, destruction

6. **Impact Strategic** — Changes to security policy, regulations, and standards
